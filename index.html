02GRAMMAR MADE FRIENDLY

Simple building blocks. Clearer conversations.

Learn one useful pattern at a time. No complicated explanations—just clear examples you can use today.

Try a practice question↗
A1

The verb “to be”

Use am, is, or are to talk about yourself and other people.

“I am new here.” Soy nuevo/a aquí.

A1

Present simple

Use the present simple for routines and everyday facts.

“I take the bus.” Tomo el autobús.

A2

Simple questions

Start with “Do you…?” to ask about routines or preferences.

“Do you need help?” ¿Necesitas ayuda?
